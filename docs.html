HandiHomepage – Documentation

Welcome to HandiHomepage – a simple, accessible dashboard designed for elderly and low‑vision users. Everything is large, touch‑friendly, and easy to navigate.

📌 Getting Started

On the main dashboard you will see several modules (boxes). Each module is a separate tool:

  • Friendly Phone – one‑tap calls to your saved contacts (lockable to prevent accidental dialling).
  • Music Player – upload your own music files, play/pause, and enjoy a visualiser (lockable).
  • Radio – live Irish radio stations with a sound synthesiser (lockable).
  • Bus Tracker – real‑time or scheduled bus arrivals (requires location).
  • Gallery – slideshow of family photos, with lightbox and screen‑wake option.
  • Emergency – sends your location to emergency contacts with one press (lockable).
  • News – latest headlines from Irish RSS feeds (scroll with ▲/▼ buttons).
  • Mastodon Social – trending posts from Mastodon (scroll with ▲/▼ buttons).

🔒 Locking Modules

Each module has a red lock button (🔒) next to the pin. When locked, the module becomes unclickable – perfect for preventing accidental calls or music changes. Click the lock to turn it green (🔓) and unlock.

📍 Pinning Modules

Use the pin button (📌) on any module to keep it at the top of your dashboard. Pinned modules stay above others, even after page reload.

⚙️ Settings & Customisation

Click the Settings icon (⚙️) in the footer bar to:

  • Upload images for the Gallery
  • Upload music files for the Music Player
  • Add / edit phone and emergency contacts
  • Choose news RSS feeds
  • Configure module visibility (enable/disable modules)

🔊 Global Mute & Screen Wake

The footer bar (always at the bottom of the screen) gives you two important controls:

  • / – Mute/Unmute all audio (radio and music) without stopping playback.
  • / – Screen Wake Lock. Sun = screen stays awake; bed = screen may sleep. (Requires browser permission.)

Other footer icons: Settings (⚙️), Documentation (📄) – this page, and Email (✉️) to contact support.

📱 Touch & Mobile

On touchscreens, drag‑and‑drop is disabled (to avoid accidental moves), but Packery still arranges modules in multiple columns if the screen is wide enough (e.g., tablets in landscape). On narrow phones, all modules stack vertically.

🚨 Emergency Button

The big red emergency button uses your device’s location and sends a text message (SMS) to all emergency contacts with an OpenStreetMap link. Always test with your own number first to ensure it works.
